Streamlined Process

How ResolveHub Operates

From lodging your grievance to verified administrative resolution in three transparent steps.

01

File Your Grievance

Sign in to your student account, select the category, provide room/location details, and submit your grievance in under 60 seconds.

02

Administrative Action

The ticket is immediately reviewed by college administration. Status dynamically updates to In Progress with assigned task forces.

03

Verified Resolution

Once on-ground action is complete, the administrator marks the grievance Resolved and logs the action in the permanent audit trail.

Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding the grievance submission and resolution lifecycle.

Who can file grievances on ResolveHub?

All enrolled students and campus staff members can register using their valid college or personal email address to submit complaints.

How will I know when my issue is being resolved?

Your dashboard updates dynamically. When the administration begins investigation or maintenance, the status changes to IN PROGRESS, and finally to RESOLVED once finished.

Can I update my student profile information?

Yes! Clicking on your profile avatar in the navigation bar opens the User Profile modal, where you can modify your display name, email address, or update your password.

How do College Administrators log in?

Staff and administrators use the official administrative email and assigned security credentials through the Sign In portal to access the Admin Console.
